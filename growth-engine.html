§ 01 · What it is

// ai.charubak

an orchestrated agent system encoding a real operator's playbook, not a prompt with my name on it

Give it a project and it produces the complete first-week work package: per-product content guidelines, a competitive recon of your actual peers (not the category giants everyone name-drops), a budget-tiered creator plan, a live narrative digest, an outside-in onboarding funnel teardown, and a compliant ads concept if (and only if) ads are even warranted for you. Every claim sourced and dated. Every document brief enough that a founder actually reads it. Everything written in one voice, because it learned mine.

The goal was never "AI marketing." The goal is an agent that does the 70% that is research and production, so the 30% that is taste, relationships, and judgment gets all of my time.
§ 02 · How it runs

// the.pipeline

four phases · same order every engagement · lessons from every run fed back in

phase_00 · intake

Understand what the job actually is

  • Maps multi-product brands (each product gets its own guidelines and competitor lens)
  • Identifies the domain crossover: Web3 x football, gaming, music — the non-crypto side gets researched with equal depth
  • Reads timing: live event window? The 30/60/90 inverts into an event-shaped sprint
  • Asks for the budget envelope before recommending a single dollar of spend
phase_01 · research

Sourced, dated, verified

  • Fetches the real product surfaces (and the domains the name implies — this once found a client's namesake domain parked for sale at $299K)
  • Five nearest-peer competitors per product, plus ecosystem allies for chain-native projects
  • Creator archetypes from both domains, priced against public CPM benchmarks
  • Narrative scan with a hard 24–48h freshness gate; unverifiable timestamp = discarded
phase_02 · package

Founder-readable deliverables

  • 700–950 words per document, tables over prose, expiry dates on perishable data
  • Built as polished .docx through a validated document pipeline
  • Estimates labeled as estimates; nothing I could not defend in the meeting
  • Voice rules enforced down to punctuation (yes, including the em-dash ban)
phase_03 · outreach

Pitch in my voice, not a template

  • Cover email drafted through a calibrated writing-voice model of how I actually write
  • Leads with the most surprising research finding, not a thank-you note
  • Names the AI leverage honestly: agents researched, I judged
  • Every run ends with a retro that updates the playbook — the agent gets better per engagement

§ 04 · What it ships

// the.package

standard contents · adapted per project · ads and SEO modules only when the diagnostic warrants them

  • Content guidelines per product — positioning, voice, terms to use/avoid, compliance language, ✓/✗ examples
  • Competitive recon — 5 nearest peers + ecosystem allies + what we take from each
  • Creator plan — budget split + 20 named targets in paid and free tiers
  • Narrative digest — a real scan of the last 48h, scored, with draft posts and ship windows
  • Funnel teardown — ranked friction hypotheses, each with its validation metric
  • Ads concept — copy, creative descriptions, test matrix, kill criteria, compliance gates
  • Outreach email — in my voice, ready to send
